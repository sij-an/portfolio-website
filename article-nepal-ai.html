What Nepal Must Do Before AI Becomes Mainstream

Sep 4, 2026 4 min read Sijan Upreti

Artificial Intelligence is no longer just a technology of the future. It is already changing education, business, healthcare, communication, and the way people work. For Nepal, AI could create new opportunities, but only if the country prepares before it becomes mainstream.

1. Focus on Early Education

The first priority should be education. Nepal needs to introduce AI, programming, data science, and digital literacy at an early stage. Universities should focus more on practical projects and research rather than only theoretical learning. Students should be encouraged to build solutions for real Nepali problems.

2. Build Digital Infrastructure

Nepal also needs better digital infrastructure. Reliable internet, accessible computing resources, and strong data systems are essential for developing and using AI. Without these foundations, Nepal may remain only a consumer of AI technologies developed by other countries.

3. Establish Data & Privacy Policies

Another major concern is data and privacy. AI depends heavily on data, so Nepal needs clear policies for how personal and public data are collected, stored, and used. Responsible AI development should protect people's privacy while still allowing innovation.

4. Invest in Research and Local Innovation

Finally, the government and private sector must invest in research and local innovation. Nepal has problems unique to its geography and society, from traffic congestion and agriculture to disaster management and healthcare. AI can help solve these problems if Nepali researchers and developers are given the resources to build local solutions.

Preparing for the Future

AI could either widen Nepal's technological gap or help the country move forward. The difference will depend on how prepared we are. Nepal does not need to compete with the world's biggest AI companies today; it needs to start building the people, infrastructure, policies, and ideas that will allow it to compete tomorrow.

Building the foundations today for a tech-driven tomorrow.
